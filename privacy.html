Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and does not collect any personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

2. Orders

When you buy from StitchSalt on Etsy, Etsy processes your order and payment under Etsy's Privacy Policy. To fulfil your order we receive your name, shipping address and order details from Etsy and share only what is needed with our print-on-demand partner (Printify and its print providers) to make and ship your item. We use this information only to fulfil your order and answer your messages, and we never sell it.

3. Our social media tools (TikTok, Pinterest)

StitchSalt's internal tools connect only to StitchSalt's own TikTok and Pinterest accounts, with the shop owner's permission, in order to upload StitchSalt's own product videos and images. Through these platforms' official APIs we access only basic profile information of our own account and the permission to upload our own content. We do not access, collect or store data about other TikTok or Pinterest users. Access tokens are stored securely on the shop owner's computer and are never shared.

4. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion of your personal data. To exercise them, message us through our Etsy shop and we will reply within 30 days.

5. Contact

Message us through our Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/StitchSalt.
